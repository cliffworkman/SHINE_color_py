    Built on SHINE

    SHINE_color — Rodrigo Dal Ben (2023), MethodsX 11, 102377. Paper ↗

    SHINE — Willenbockel, Sadr, Fiset, Horne, Gosselin & Tanaka (2010), Behavior Research Methods 42, 671–684. Paper ↗

    SHINE_color_py continues the original toolbox through independently validated Python behavior. Maintenance and Python implementation: Cliff Workman, with substantial OpenAI Codex assistance under human direction and review.

    Review feature: motivated by Vasiliki and Cliff’s architectural-stimulus review; proposed by Cliff Workman and elaborated with ChatGPT. Interaction design follows Cliff Workman’s stimulus-upscaler reviewer. This implementation is independently written.

    Metrics describe transformations, not scientific quality. L/V are SHINE working channels on 0–255; SD is sample SD. Saved-output statistics include quantization and RGB reconstruction. No display color calibration is implied. Preview RGB follows the recorded decoder policy without ICC/gamma conversion.

    Phase 1: modes 1–2 only. Spectral diagnostics, metadata joins, cross-run comparisons and SSIM are not implemented. Original licensing and component notices remain in the repository.
